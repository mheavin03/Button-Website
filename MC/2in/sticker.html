Instructions

2in Sticker

To start either click inside a circle to add an image or click folder_open , to add multiple images at once.

Tools:

  • - delete the image from the circle

  • - make image larger

  • - make image smaller

  • - reset image to original size and position

  • - duplicate image to next circle

Once you have the template filled out, click the download button at the top right of the screen and email mediacenter@harding.edu the template!
